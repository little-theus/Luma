What is Luma?

Luma is a reflection and self-care app. It helps you record difficult moments, look back on what happened, and recognize patterns over time — so you can understand yourself a little better and prepare for what comes next.

Luma is not a diagnostic or treatment tool. It's a companion for reflection, meant to sit quietly alongside the people and professionals who already support you.

The Luma journey

Five steps, always in your own time

  1. 01

    Record

    Capture what happened, how intense it felt, and what came with it.

  2. 02

    Reflect

    Look back with a clearer mind, away from the moment itself.

  3. 03

    Recognize

    Notice patterns forming across your own history.

  4. 04

    Prepare

    Turn what you've learned into a plan for next time.

  5. 05

    Continue

    Keep going — one entry, one day at a time.

Features

Small tools for a steady practice

Records

Register moments, intensity, thoughts, sensations, reactions and what followed.

My Tree

A visual representation of your journey's continuity — it grows as you keep showing up.

Calm Bubble

A simple pause for reflection, with gentle messages and visual change.

Echoes

Freely register thoughts or moments to come back to later.

Prevention Plan

Organize your own strategies for moments of rising pressure or difficulty.

Support Network

Add people you trust and reach them easily by WhatsApp, call, or text.

Reminders

Set gentle nudges to help you keep a steady reflection routine.

Privacy

Designed with privacy in mind

Luma's core features are built to work locally, right on your device — no account and no email address required to start reflecting.

Your entries are stored locally on your device. No approach can promise absolute security, but privacy is treated as a design principle from the start, not an afterthought.
